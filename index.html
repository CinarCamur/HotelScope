HotelScope

AI-Powered Multi-Channel Hotel Review Analysis and Automated Department Routing System

Çınar Çamur · Doğu Ata Ertürk · Bora Yaver

HotelScope is an AI-powered platform designed to streamline hotel feedback management by collecting guest reviews from
multiple channels, such as Google, Booking.com, TripAdvisor, and other feedback sources. Using AI-driven classification,
the system automatically analyzes and categorizes reviews based on their content, sentiment, and relevant hotel departments.

The platform aims to simplify the feedback management process by grouping similar complaints, identifying recurring
issues, and automatically routing feedback to the appropriate departments. By reducing manual workload and improving
response efficiency, HotelScope helps hotels address guest concerns more effectively and enhance overall service quality.
